## Description
GroundLoop helps you turn unavoidable food scraps into compost that reaches local soil — and makes that journey easy to join and possible to verify.

- Sort correctly for your city. Clear Yes / No / Share it / It depends answers from each city's official programme.
- Find where to compost. Drop-off sites on a map, with directions in your maps app.
- Keep a weekly streak. One drop-off a week keeps it going; it survives travel and pauses instead of breaking.
- Prove it, your way. Self-report, add a photo, or confirm your location — proof levels are honest and shown everywhere.
- Share progress. A friendly community and a season recap you can share as an image.

GroundLoop rewards consistency and careful sorting — never how much waste you make.

Privacy: we read your location only at the moment you log a drop-off, never in the background, and photos are taken by you when you choose to add proof.
