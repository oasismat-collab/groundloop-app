Privacy promise

Privacy is a promise, not a setting.

GroundLoop only works if you trust it. These rules bind every feature we build — if something can't satisfy them, it doesn't ship. Here's what we do with your data, in plain language.

The short version: residents are free forever, we never sell your personal data, and partners only ever see anonymized totals for an area — never you.

What we never do

  • We never sell personal data. Not your name, your email, your photos, your locations or your history — to partners or to anyone else.
  • Partners never see you. No partner view ever shows a name, a username, a proof photo, an exact location, an individual history, or an exact drop-off time.
  • We don't track where you go. Your location is read only at the moment you check in a drop-off — never followed in the background.

What partners can see

Cities, haulers and companies pay for insight, not people. They see aggregated data only:

  • Counts and trends for an area — drop-offs per site, participation over time, the mix of proof quality.
  • Where residents have asked for a bin, and a ranked list of underserved areas.
  • Coverage: how many people a set of sites serves, in aggregate.

Site coordinates are approximate, and resident bin requests are coarsened (rounded) before they're ever aggregated.

The 10-user minimum

An area is shown to partners only if at least 10 opted-in people contribute to it. If fewer than 10 people in an area are sharing, that area is hidden or merged into a neighbouring one until the combined area clears 10. This applies to every breakdown — maps, site stats, trend points — so no one can be singled out, even indirectly. Where an area is hidden, the dashboard says so, so you can see the guarantee at work.

Sharing is opt-in, and off by default

  • Helping your city with your anonymous activity is opt-in and off by default. The toggle in the app reads: "Share my anonymous activity to help my city add drop-off sites."
  • Only opted-in activity is ever counted toward a partner aggregate.
  • If you opt out, you're removed from every future aggregate — and you keep every resident feature, free, exactly as before.

Proof photos stay with verification

Photos you add as proof of a drop-off are used only to verify that drop-off. They are a resident-only, verification-only artefact and never reach a partner view.
