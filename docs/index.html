A transparent estimate, not a promise

Route: Managed aerobic composting → finished compost → soil. Process version: illustrative-v1.

Illustrative scenario assumptions based on EPA composting research: N 70%, P 93%, K 85% retained. Ca, Mg, Fe, Zn assumed 95% retained (relatively stable minerals). Finished material applied 100%. No illustrative carbon or organic-matter factors.

Each amount = fresh component mass × fresh-weight concentration × its element-specific retention × fraction applied. Dry-weight values are converted using dry-matter fraction first. Retention already accounts for losses; no extra compost-yield multiplier is applied.

portion-v2-expanded: Typical serving sizes are based on USDA standard reference portions and common household measures. Individual portions vary; all values are editable. Database includes 60 foods with nutritional data from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy.

Calcium, magnesium, sulfur and trace-element returns remain unknown without validated retention factors. Sodium is not counted as a soil benefit.

What these cards don’t measure

Carbon returned is carbon applied in finished compost: it is different from carbon originally in food, carbon remaining years later, or net greenhouse-gas emissions avoided. It is not permanent sequestration.

Food moisture, compost moisture at application, soil water management and water used to grow food are separate. Much food moisture evaporates. We do not estimate litres saved or water returned; composting does not recover growing water.

Leaves or wood may be needed. Their mass, carbon and nutrients are excluded from the food contribution.

Missing values stay unknown, never zero. Feedipedia data is excluded pending reuse permission. No pomace or mixed citrus pulp substitutes are used.
