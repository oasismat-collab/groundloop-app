The problem

Good scraps keep ending up in the wrong place.

Food is the heaviest thing most of us throw away — and it doesn't have to be waste at all.

🗑️

Scraps go to landfill

Buried food scraps rot without air and release methane, instead of becoming compost for local soil.

🤔

No one's sure what's compostable

Rules change from city to city. People guess, give up, or contaminate the bin.

🗺️

Cities can't see the gaps

Programme managers don't know which neighbourhoods want a drop-off site and have nowhere to go.

For residents

How it works

Four small steps that fit into a normal week.

Sort it

Ask "can it go in?" and get a city-specific answer in a tap.

Drop it

Find a nearby drop-off site on the map and log your visit.

Keep your streak

Compost once a week to keep your streak — it travels with you to any city.

See your recap

Look back at your season: drop-offs, longest streak, where your scraps went.

Meet Peel the banana.

Peel the banana rots if you stop — getting spotty, then brown — and turns fresh again the moment you log a drop-off. A gentle mood, never a guilt trip.

Privacy promise

Privacy is a promise, not a setting.

The whole model depends on trust, so these rules bind every feature.

  • ✓We never sell your personal data — not your name, photos, locations or history.
  • ✓Partners only ever see aggregates — totals and trends for an area, never you.
  • ✓Any area with fewer than 10 opted-in people is hidden, so no one can be singled out.
  • ✓Sharing is opt-in only and off by default. Opt out any time and you leave every aggregate.
